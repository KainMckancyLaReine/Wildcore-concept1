Trail running in Spain, just outside Valencia

Most people know Valencia for its city, beaches and paella. But less than an hour inland there is a very different Valencia: the mountains. The Sierra Calderona Natural Park is full of pine forests, red sandstone, gorges and viewpoints, with trails for every level. That is where Wildcore Retreats runs its trail running weekends.

This is a retreat, not a training camp. We run by feel, stop where it is beautiful and stay together as a group. Walking the steep bits is always fine. Our motto: don't perform, experience.

Who is it for?

Anyone aged 18+ who can run easily for at least an hour and a half. No trail experience is needed. You can come alone or with a friend; groups are a maximum of six people, so everyone gets attention.

The three routes of the weekend

Friday

Casinos Discovery Loop

8 km · ±180 m gain

An easy first loop on the trails around Casinos, through pine forest and rolling mountain paths.

Saturday

Serra Calderona Grand Loop

20–23 km · ±850 m gain

The big day: a long route through the Sierra Calderona past viewpoints, with lunch out in nature.

Sunday

Rebalsadors Recovery Loop

13–15 km · ±500 m gain

A gentler finish past gorges and waterfalls, then lunch on the terrace and the airport transfer.

Routes may be adjusted for weather, wildfire risk or safety.

Your base: a villa with a pool

You stay at Villa El Pinar, a detached villa near the village of Casinos, surrounded by greenery with views of the mountains. After the trail: a swim, the terrace, good food and good company. Or some time on your own, if you prefer.

What's included

  • Two nights at Villa El Pinar (shared twin room or glamping tent)
  • 2 breakfasts, 3 lunches and 2 dinners, plus coffee, tea and water
  • Transfer to and from Valencia Airport
  • Transport to all trailheads
  • Guidance on every run from host Maurizio Salazar, sports and shiatsu therapist

Not included: your flight to Valencia and travel insurance (which is required).

Why trail running in Valencia in winter?

The weekends run from November to March. While northern Europe is wet and dark, inland Valencia usually offers pleasant running weather, dry trails and long views. Summer is too hot for long trail days, which makes winter the ideal season.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need trail running experience?

No. You just need to be able to run easily for at least 90 minutes. We walk the steep sections and stay together as a group.

How do I get there?

Fly into Valencia Airport. We pick the group up on Friday around 1 pm and drive to the villa near Casinos. On Sunday afternoon we take you back to the airport.

How much does the trail running retreat cost?

The first edition (6–8 November 2026) costs €495 pp as a launch price. After that it is €595 pp, including accommodation, meals, airport transfers and guidance.

Is it in English?

Yes. Your host speaks Dutch, English and Spanish.

Why Wildcore

This weekend isn't about performing.
It's about experiencing.

For us, trail running isn't about speed or competition. It's about being outdoors, moving in nature, and noticing what happens once you step away from the rush of everyday life.

In the Sierra Calderona we run by feel. No strict schedule, no focus on times or performance. We stop where it's beautiful, look out over the valley, take a photo, or simply stand still in nature for a moment.

Trail run or hike? The choice is yours. Every route can be done as a trail run or as a hike, so you decide what suits you best. Whether you love to run or would rather take in the scenery on foot, the experience always comes first. We stay together as a group.

"Not performing. Experiencing."

01

Who is this weekend for?

This weekend is meant for active people who love being outdoors. You can join as a trail runner or as a hiker. No experience is required, but a good base level of fitness is important. We therefore ask participants to be able to run or walk comfortably for at least an hour and a half on hilly terrain.

The group has a maximum of six participants. It's exactly this small scale that allows for personal guidance, attention for everyone and a relaxed atmosphere.

Whether you come on your own or with someone else, you are very welcome. Together we'll make it a sporty, relaxed and inspiring weekend, with nature, movement and good company at its heart.

Not about performing, but about experiencing it together.

02

A villa with a pool

You'll be staying in my detached villa in El Pinar, a quiet spot in the middle of nature with a pool, spacious terraces and views of the mountains. The perfect place to fully unwind after a day on the trails.

03

Pine forests & rolling mountain trails

We run through breathtaking landscapes: fragrant pine forests, rolling mountain trails and surprising little paths you'd never find on your own. Every route has its own atmosphere and pace.

04

Views you won't forget any time soon

The highlight: your first confident trail run through the Spanish landscape. With viewpoints over ravines, valleys and rock formations that stay with you long after you're back home.

05

Sport, rest & good company

A perfect combination of physical challenge, relaxation and connection. After the runs: good food, good conversation, and time to really get to know each other, in a deliberately small group.

Weekend schedule

Three days, three routes

Each day has its own character, from a gentle start to the highlight of the weekend and a relaxed recovery loop to enjoy the afterglow.

Friday

Casinos Sunset Loop

8 km±180 m

A gentle start. We explore the trails around Casinos as the sun sets over the valley, perfect for getting used to the terrain and getting to know each other.

  1. 14:00 – 16:00
    Welcome in Casinos

    You arrive at the villa in El Pinar, where you can check in at your own pace and pick your room. Take your time to meet the other participants, enjoy a coffee on the terrace and let the journey fall away.

  2. 18:00
    Sunset Trail

    We open the weekend with a relaxed trail run around Casinos. A beautiful route over rolling paths and through pine forests, at an easy pace with plenty of time for the first views.

  3. 20:00
    Dinner together

    After the trail we sit down to a healthy, flavourful meal. A relaxed evening to get to know each other better.

Saturday

Serra Calderona Grand Loop

20–23 km±850 m

The heart of the weekend: a longer route through breathtaking landscapes and past viewpoints you won't soon forget. Walking is always allowed.

  1. 07:00 – 08:00
    Breakfast

    We start the day with a generous, nourishing breakfast so everyone has enough energy for the finest trail of the weekend.

  2. 09:00
    Departure for the main trail

    The impressive route through the Sierra Calderona, over varied mountain paths, through fragrant pine forests and past spectacular viewpoints. Along the way we take time for photos and lunch together out in nature.

  3. 16:00
    Back at the villa

    Time to unwind: a refreshing dip in the pool, a book on the terrace, or simply the quiet and the view over the mountains.

  4. 20:00
    Dinner together

    Another healthy, flavourful meal and a relaxed evening, looking ahead to tomorrow's trail.

Sunday

Rebalsadors Recovery Loop

13–15 km±500 m

A calmer finish along ravines and waterfalls, with plenty of time to soak it all in before heading home.

  1. 07:00 – 08:00
    Breakfast

    One more breakfast together before we head into the mountains for a relaxed close to the weekend.

  2. 09:00
    Morning trail

    A varied route through the Sierra Calderona where we enjoy the quiet, the nature and the wide views one last time.

  3. 13:00
    Lunch together

    Back at the villa we close the weekend with a convivial lunch on the terrace. A lovely moment to share experiences and savour it all.

  4. 15:00
    Farewell

    After a weekend full of nature, sport, relaxation and new encounters we say goodbye, with fresh energy, inspiration and perhaps a new love for trail running.

Your home base

Villa El Pinar, near Casinos

During this weekend you won't stay in a hotel, but in an atmospheric, detached villa that feels like a home away from home, in the quiet urbanisation of El Pinar, right next to the authentic village of Casinos.

The villa sits amid greenery with views over the mountains of the Sierra Calderona. After a day on the trails you unwind by the pool, on the terrace or in the garden. Inside, it's all about a warm, homely atmosphere: coffee in the morning, stories after the trail run, and healthy, wholesome meals without any rush.

  • Pool & sun terrace
  • Panoramic mountain views
  • Shared living space
  • Large garden to unwind in
  • Breakfast, lunch & dinner included
  • Room for everyone, no rush
About your host

Maurizio Salazar

My name is Maurizio Salazar and I live for much of the year in the beautiful surroundings of Casinos, at the foot of the Sierra Calderona.

By profession I'm a sports and shiatsu therapist, and I've been fascinated by movement, health and the power of nature my whole life. Sport has always played an important role for me. Whether it's running, trail running, hiking or any other kind of movement, I believe being active is good not only for the body but for the mind as well.

Through my work I'm in daily contact with people who want to live healthier, stronger and with more energy. It shows me again and again how important it is to step out of your daily routine now and then and make time for yourself.

Nature has always been the place where I recharge. In the mountains of the Sierra Calderona I find calm, freedom and adventure. That feeling is what I want to share with others.

Out of that passion came the idea of organising small-scale trail running weekends. Not a performance-driven sports trip, but an accessible experience where movement, nature, relaxation and connection take centre stage.

I'd like to invite you to discover the trails, the surroundings and the relaxed Spanish way of life. Together we enjoy beautiful routes, healthy meals, good conversation and the unique atmosphere of my home in El Pinar.

— Maurizio

Who is this weekend for?

Open to anyone aged 18 or over with a good base level of fitness. You are responsible for assessing your own physical condition and health. The group is kept deliberately small, so there's room for personal attention.

What's included?

Accommodation at the villa, breakfast, lunch, 2 shared dinners, airport transfer from Valencia Airport, transfers to the trailheads and guidance on every trail run and hike.

Accommodation

During the trail running weekend you stay in our characterful villa in El Pinar, surrounded by the nature of Casinos. You sleep in a shared twin room or, if you prefer to be closer to nature, in a comfortable glamping tent among the pine trees, also suitable for two people.

Whether you choose the villa or the glamping tent, you'll enjoy a calm, homely setting where relaxation, comfort and nature come together. A wonderful place to fully recharge after a day on the trails.

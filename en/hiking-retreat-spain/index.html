Hiking in Valencia: the mountains behind the city

Valencia is best known for its city and beaches, but the best hiking is inland. The Sierra Calderona, a protected natural park since 2002, has pine and cork-oak forests, red sandstone rock, old paths and viewpoints where you can see all the way to the Mediterranean on a clear day.

On a Wildcore hiking weekend everything is taken care of. We pick you up at the airport, you stay in a villa with a pool, we eat together and each day you hike a different route with a guide. It is not about speed. It is about being outside and slowing down.

Who is this hiking retreat for?

Anyone aged 18+ in reasonable shape who can hike for at least three hours on hilly terrain. You do not need to be an experienced mountain hiker. Because each weekend is either a hiking or a trail running weekend, the whole group moves at the same pace.

The three routes of the weekend

Friday

Casinos Discovery Loop

8 km · ±180 m gain

An easy first loop on the trails around Casinos, through pine forest and rolling mountain paths.

Saturday

Serra Calderona Grand Loop

20–23 km · ±850 m gain

The big day: a long route through the Sierra Calderona past viewpoints, with lunch out in nature.

Sunday

Rebalsadors Recovery Loop

13–15 km · ±500 m gain

A gentler finish past gorges and waterfalls, then lunch on the terrace and the airport transfer.

The hiking weekend follows the same three-day structure in the same area, at walking pace. Distances may vary per edition due to weather, wildfire risk or safety. Feel free to ask for details.

Why a hiking weekend in winter?

The hiking weekends take place in December and March, when inland Valencia is usually sunny and mild enough for comfortable hiking. In summer it is often too hot for long days on the trail. A long weekend is enough: fly in on Friday morning, be home on Sunday night.

Frequently asked questions

Is this hiking retreat suitable for beginners?

Yes, if you have a good base level of fitness and can hike for at least three hours on hilly terrain. No mountain experience needed.

When are the next hiking weekends?

Can I join on my own?

Absolutely. Many people come alone. With a maximum of six participants, you get to know each other quickly.

What if the weekend does not go ahead?

A minimum of four participants is needed. You will know at least four weeks before departure, and if we cancel you get a full refund.
